% Figure 1.1, band A --- the tetrahedral panel of Figure 2.2, re-drawn.
%
% Same macros, same pose {60}{120}, one notch smaller: \PanelScale 2.47 against
% Figure 2.2's 2.6, which is why the scale is a macro in the shared preamble
% rather than a literal (renewing it here leaves Figure 2.2 byte-identical).
% Line widths are absolute and do not scale, and 5% is invisible, so nothing is
% compensated for.  \PanelFooter is deliberately NOT used: the solid's name is
% the band header on the page, and the footer would cost ~17pt to repeat it.
%
% The four numbered chips are placed AFTER the scaled scope closes, so their
% 9pt/11pt offsets are true page points; ($(tk)!-9pt!(O)$) reads "9pt beyond
% the tip, along the ray from the sphere centre", which is projection-agnostic
% and must not be replaced by hard-coded angles.  Vertex 1 points nearly at the
% camera, so its blade is a foreshortened stub and its chip needs the larger
% offset.  The three axis names are grey and knocked out in white because
% \hat x lands on vertex 4's blade; they name the FRAME, not outcomes, and
% Pauli ket labels are forbidden on this panel (see \AxisNubs' comment in
% _povm_sphere_preamble.tex --- the octahedron alone keeps the kets).
%
% Include at natural size --- no width= --- the type set here is printed type.
\input{_povm_sphere_preamble}
